Questions

Does DealShield read my whole inbox?

No. It only reads the email thread you have open, and only sends it for analysis when you click Analyze. Nothing else in your inbox is accessed.

What do you store?

Your account email, plan and monthly usage, plus your analysis history: the subject, the other person's name and email, and the generated drafts. The email body itself is never stored. You can delete any analysis from the popup. See the privacy policy.

Will it make up prices or deadlines?

It's designed not to. A fact-check step compares every draft with the email and turns anything it can't verify into a [placeholder] for you to fill in. Always review a draft before sending.

Does it work with Google Workspace accounts?

Yes. It works in Gmail on the web (mail.google.com) in Chrome, for personal and Workspace accounts.

How do I cancel Pro?

Click Cancel in the extension popup. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
